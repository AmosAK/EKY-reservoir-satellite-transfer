# Data licence

The data files in `data/` and `gis/` are released under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The field measurements are derived from public records in the Water Quality Portal
(https://www.waterqualitydata.us) and the U.S. EPA National Lakes Assessment. Please
also cite those sources when reusing the data.
